# Grill / Red-Team Mode

Purpose: attempt to falsify or materially weaken the current research contract before more resources are committed.

## Attack order

1. Is the research problem real and important enough?
2. Is the claimed gap supported by primary literature?
3. Is the contribution actually distinct from prior work?
4. Is the proposed mechanism causal, explanatory, or merely decorative?
5. Could the same expected result arise from a simpler alternative explanation?
6. Can the planned experiment distinguish those explanations?
7. Are baselines and controls fair?
8. What result would make the project fail?
9. If everything works as expected, is the publication story still meaningful?
10. What is the highest-value unresolved risk?

## Output

- strongest surviving proposition
- fatal or near-fatal weaknesses
- experiments/evidence required for survival
- useful but optional improvements
- verdict: `SURVIVES | SURVIVES_WITH_CONDITIONS | PIVOT | KILL`
